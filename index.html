THE RESEARCH TAKEAWAY

Start with the return.
Leave room for the interest.

Among the unambiguous rates checked, Marcus and Barclays both pay 3.50% below $250,000. Barclays adds a conditional $200 cash offer; Marcus offers a referral-only rate boost. Neither is an automatic entitlement.

Depositing $250,000 uses the full single-owner FDIC limit before interest. Under a constant 3.50% scenario, a $240,000 deposit grows to $248,400 in one year, before any bonus.

01 / HIGHEST RETURN

Promotions change the comparison.

For an eligible new customer, Barclays’ $200 adds 0.80% of a $25,000 deposit, but only 0.08% of $250,000. Marcus’ boost is temporary and needs a qualifying referral. Compare conditions and time horizons, not headline rates alone.

02 / PROTECTION FIRST

A bank brand is not an insurance bucket.

Same-bank deposits in the same ownership category aggregate. Capital One says newly opened Discover deposits count with Capital One deposits. Splitting across those brands does not create extra capacity.
