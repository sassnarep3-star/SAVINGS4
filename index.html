EXECUTIVE TAKEAWAY

Earn $9,500–$10,500/yr per insured slot — not $25 at a branch mega-bank.

America's largest branch banks (#1 Chase 0.01% NYC PDF, #4 Wells Way2Save 0.01%) pay $25/year on $250,000, while #2 Bank of America and #3 Citibank mask live rates behind stateful ZIP sessions. Moving reserve cash to nationally chartered digital divisions of major banks multiplies annual yield more than 350× with zero monthly fees and full mobile security.

Critical FDIC rule: Deposit insurance caps principal plus accrued interest at $250,000 per owner per charter. Deposit ~$239,800–$241,500 for a 1-year single-owner account, open a $500,000 joint account, or split across distinct national charters.
