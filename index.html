🏆 Verdict (verified, Oct 22, 2026)

Best all-round big-bank choice: Synchrony (3.55% APY) for a flat rate on any balance with no conditions, or Marcus by Goldman Sachs (3.50% APY) — nearly identical, no minimum/fees, plus an official referral boost (+1.00% APY for 3 months).
Best at exactly $250k: Barclays Tiered (3.65% at the $250k tier) — but depositing the full $250,000 leaves your interest uninsured in a single-ownership account (see Max FDIC Strategy).
Best if you want more than $250k insured at one login: SoFi — official Insured Deposit Program insures up to $3M via participating banks; base savings APY 3.30% only with eligible direct deposit / $5,000 qualifying deposits every 31 days, otherwise 0.80%.
Avoid for yield: Chase Savings and standard Wells Fargo / Bank of America savings pay 0.01%–0.04% territory — at $250,000 that is about $25–$100 a year vs $8,875 at Synchrony.
