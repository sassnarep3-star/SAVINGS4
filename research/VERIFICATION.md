# Verification record — 2026-10-22 UTC

## Method and scope

Repository started with only `README.md`; no pre-existing data to verify. Official bank product pages, disclosures, FAQs, security pages, and FDIC guidance were read using the page retrieval tool in this session. Relevant sections were retrieved beyond the first chunk for Ally, American Express, Barclays, Marcus, PNC, Synchrony, and Bank of America. Evidence selections are recorded in `data/sources.json`; they are selected passages/transcriptions, not full archival captures or independently signed bank quotes. Read dates and publisher rate dates are separate. Sources are not silently refreshed by the site.

12 product entries across 11 bank groups are covered: Ally, American Express, Bank of America, Barclays, Capital One (with Discover redirect noted), Chase, Citi, Marcus/Goldman Sachs, PNC, and Synchrony, and Wells Fargo (two products). This is a bounded national-bank shortlist, not an exhaustive list of every US savings product or an independently asset-ranked census. “National digital” describes product distribution for this study, not a federal bank-charter classification. No small local banks, fintech pass-through programs, brokerage sweeps, checking bundles, money markets or CDs are ranked as savings accounts.

## Cross-checks and limitations

- FDIC own guidance confirms $250,000 per depositor/bank/category; own FAQ confirms accrued interest is included. Existing same-bank checking/CD/savings reduce capacity. Single-owner/no-beneficiary/no-other-deposits is a modeling assumption, not a fact about the user. No user identity or deposits are known.
- Direct FDIC October 19 national savings rate 0.38% used rather than inconsistent/older bank marketing averages. FDIC national rate is a weighted benchmark, not a best-market quote.
- Missing promotion values remain JSON null and empty numerical cells in rendered tables with accessible unverified labels. A blank never means no offer exists.
- No account was opened and no deposit made. No application, credentials, referral, date of birth, residence, ownership structure, tax status, existing balance or offer eligibility is assumed to have been verified.
- Official pages can be dynamic, region-dependent or stale. Successful retrieval is not a universal guarantee a link works in every browser. Broken diagnostic URLs are omitted from navigation and retained here as investigative history only.
- Future rates, taxes, inflation, and bank-specific daily rounding are not forecast. Calculation methods and assumptions are published separately, never represented as official bank quotes.
