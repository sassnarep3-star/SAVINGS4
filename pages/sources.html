Sources & methodology

Checked October 22, 2026 (UTC). Evidence selections, publisher dates, calculation assumptions and limitations are published for manual review. No third-party ranking is used as a bank-origin rate quote.

What “verified” means here

A field was read on the linked official bank or FDIC page. Publisher dates are kept distinct from the date checked. This is a manual, field-level research snapshot—not independent certification, a security audit, a signed bank quote or real-time data.

Evidence excerpts are selected passages/transcriptions, not complete archived pages. Official content can change, be personalized, be stale, or fail to render.

Calculation contract

Modeled returns are intentionally labeled, so they cannot be confused with verified financial facts.

growth = (1 + APY / 100) ^ (actual elapsed days / 365)
interest = principal × (growth − 1)
headroom principal = floor_to_cents[(249,999 / growth) − reserved cash bonus]
  • Opening and immediate full funding are assumed October 22, 2026. Horizons are January 22, April 22 and October 22, 2027: 92, 182 and 365 days. These are calendar calculations, not bank deadlines.
  • APY already reflects compounding. Do not divide APY by 365 and compound again. Fractional-year APY powers are an approximation; bank day-count, posting, funding holds and rounding can change actual earnings.
  • APYs stay constant only for illustration. No new deposits, withdrawals or taxes; no unverified bonuses included in base returns. Confirmed modeled products have no monthly maintenance fee; optional service fees are not modeled.
  • FDIC example: one depositor, one ownership category, no other same-bank deposits, no assumed beneficiaries. $1 reserve beyond flooring reduces rounding risk; it does not protect against rate changes or unknown deposits.
  • Barclays uses 3.50% below $250,000. The $250,000+ tier cannot be retained-interest fully insured under this scenario. Headroom table conservatively reserves $200 at day zero; actual bonus payment is delayed and conditional.
  • Marcus referral model is a separate hypothetical successful-referral scenario: Oct 22 at 3.50%, Oct 23–Jan 23 (92 days) at 4.50%, then 3.50%. Assumes one-day activation; actual timing can differ. Base models do not assume a referral.
  • Barclays 12-month offer comparison adds $200 only if paid by then, with no modeled interest on the cash. Total gain / deposit is a simple scenario return, not a quoted APY.
  • Benchmark uses the direct FDIC October 19 savings national rate of 0.38%. It is deposit-weighted, not an offer available to open or the regulatory rate cap. Percentage-point spreads are calculations, not bank marketing claims.
  • Missing values remain null in JSON and empty in numeric cells, with accessible labels and review status. No exact ZIP, personal eligibility or existing banking relationship is assumed.
