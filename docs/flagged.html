What “needs review” means

It does NOT mean the number is wrong — it means we couldn't pull it directly from the official bank's static page on our cutoff fetch, so we used a closely observed secondary check and left a trail. You can resolve most flags in 30 seconds by opening the link, checking the APY and date, and telling us to update.

How to request a change: Open an issue or PR at github.com/sassnarep3-star/SAVINGS4 with the bank, the official link, and the corrected APY + date you see. We'll re-verify and mark it done in COORDINATION.md.
