Why max FDIC matters

FDIC insures $250,000 per depositor, per bank, per ownership category (fdic.gov). So:

  • 1 person, 1 bank, single account: $250k covered. $251k = $1k uninsured.
  • Joint account (2 owners): $500k covered ($250k each co-owner).
  • Trust with 2 beneficiaries: $500k single-owner coverage.
  • 2 different banks: $250k × 2 = $500k (separate limits).
Strategy: Want $500k–$1M fully FDIC-insured? Split across 2–4 big banks in our table — all are separate FDIC charters. Example: $250k at Marcus (3.50%) = $8,750/yr + $250k at CIT (3.75%) = $9,375/yr → $18,125/yr on $500k, fully covered at two banks.

FDIC-max strategy — how to keep $500k–$1M fully insured

  • Single + Joint: You + partner open single ($250k each) + joint ($500k) at one bank = $1M covered across categories.
  • Trust + beneficiaries: $250k per unique beneficiary. 1 owner × 3 beneficiaries = $750k covered in revocable trust at one bank.
  • Multiple banks: $250k at Marcus + $250k at CIT + $250k at Barclays = $750k (separate charters). All three are different FDIC certs.
  • Watch the Discover trap: Discover merged into Capital One May 18, 2025 — they now share one $250k limit. Don't split between them expecting 2× coverage.
