FDIC insurance, apps, and security — bank by bank

Per your request: every recommended bank must have a digital app, strong security, and FDIC insurance. This page proves it for each one — with links you can verify yourself.

FDIC coverage $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, for each ownership category. All deposits in the same category at one bank are added together. Different banks = separate $250k. Source: FDIC Understanding Deposit Insurance + Deposits at a Glance. Verify any bank at FDIC BankFind.

Single account

Your personal savings + checking + CDs in your name only at one bank = one $250k cap. $300k total = $50k uninsured.

$250,000 per owner

Joint account

Joint with one other person = $500k covered ($250k per co-owner). Separate from personal single $250k — can stack.

$500,000 2 owners

Trust / beneficiaries

Revocable trust = $250k per unique beneficiary. 1 owner × 4 beneficiaries = $1M at one bank.

$250k × beneficiaries
