What you need (standard checklist)

  1. Full name, date of birth, mailing address (not P.O. Box for some), email, phone
  2. SSN/ITIN, citizenship/residency, employment + annual income (Capital One asks explicitly)
  3. Agree to terms → approve → fund within window (Capital One 60 days; Citi/Ally no deadline; PNC 180 days or closed)
  4. Link external bank for ACH (1–3 business days; trial deposits) or mail a check
  5. Set up beneficiaries / joint owner if you want ownership-category FDIC boost

All banks listed are FDIC-insured — verify at FDIC BankFind. CD early withdrawal penalties reduce earnings (e.g., 90 days interest for ≤1yr terms).

Our recommendation by goal

  • Max dollars on max FDIC with no hoops: Marcus 3.50% — open in 5 min, $0, refer friend for +1% 3 mo.
  • Squeeze every basis point: CIT + BOOST50 → 4.25% for 6 mo, then 3.75% (but feed it $5k+).
  • Keep one branch bank: Keep Chase/BofA checking for daily, but move savings to Marcus/CIT — keeping $250k at 0.01% costs $9,350/yr vs CIT.
  • Need $250k+ FDIC at one bank? Use joint + trust owners — see Security & Apps for FDIC math.
