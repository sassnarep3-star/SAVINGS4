💡 If you want max FDIC + max yield — what to do

  1. Don't put $250k in Chase/BofA/Wells standard. You'd earn $25–$100/yr vs $7,500–$9,375 elsewhere — and forfeit the entire point of saving.
  2. For pure yield + FDIC-max: CIT 3.75% (or 4.25% boost 6 mo) or Barclays Tiered 3.65% on $250k+ are the mathematical winners in this big-bank set.
  3. For no-minimum simplicity: Marcus 3.50% is the cleanest — $0, no tier, no relationship hoops, verified Oct 21.
  4. Need a branch + yield? Capital One 360 3.10% hybrid or U.S. Bank Smartly up to 3.50% IF you add Smartly Checking (0.05% without).
  5. Want more than $250k FDIC at one bank? Use ownership categories: personal + joint + trust/beneficiary. Or split across 2 banks — 2× $250k = $500k covered. Learn how →
