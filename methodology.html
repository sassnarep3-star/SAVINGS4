Lifespan reading: (1) Every savings APY here is variable — each bank's official page reserves the right to change it at any time, so 3- and 5-year figures are constant-rate projections, not locked returns. (2) The longer the horizon, the more the APY gap compounds: at $250,000 the 5-year gap between Synchrony (3.55%) and the FDIC average (0.38%) is ≈ $42,853, and vs Chase 0.01% ≈ $47,514 (see Returns, Table 2). (3) If you need the rate locked for a known lifespan, a CD is the bank product that does that — e.g. Barclays Online CDs listed at 4.50% (12-month) and 4.75% (60-month) APY as of 10/07/2026 on its official compare page, with early-withdrawal penalties. (4) Conditional rates decay: SoFi's 3.30% requires ongoing qualifying activity every 31 days (else 0.80%), its 4.40% boost ends after ≤6 months, Wells' 3.25% requires keeping ≥$25,000 and being a new customer, and Marcus' +1.00% referral boost ends after 3 months — always compare the post-promotion rate for long holding periods.

Calculation method

  • Interest = Deposit × ((1 + APY)years − 1); balances = deposit + interest. APY already embeds each bank's compounding, so no separate compounding assumption is added.
  • Barclays Tiered uses the whole-balance tier stated by Barclays (“APY earned is based on the Tier into which your end of day account balance falls”).
  • SoFi Plus row: 4.70% on the first $20,000 + 3.30% on the remainder (individual account, per the Oct 20, 2026 rate sheet), before any subscription cost.
  • Max fully-insured deposit = $250,000 ÷ (1+APY)years (FDIC covers principal + accrued interest).
  • Excluded: taxes, fees (other than stated monthly fees), bonus taxes, and any rate changes after 2026-10-22.
