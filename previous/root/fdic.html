Maximising FDIC insurance for your savings

Goal stated in the brief: the maximum FDIC insurance for savings. Key fact most people miss: the $250,000 limit covers principal + accrued interest, so depositing exactly $250,000 leaves the interest uninsured in a single-ownership account at one bank.

Practical max-FDIC plan (from the verified rates): if you want every dollar insured at one institution with one login, SoFi's SIDP (up to $3M) is the only verified program in this study that does that — at 3.30% APY only if you keep eligible direct deposit / $5,000 qualifying deposits flowing every 31 days (else 0.80%). If you prefer no conditions, spread ~$241k (1-yr ceiling) each across Synchrony (3.55%), Marcus (3.50%) and Barclays/Amex/Capital One, re-checking ceilings as rates change.
