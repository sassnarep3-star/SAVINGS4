Competitiveness over the lifespan: the gap compounds. At a constant APY, $250,000 earns about $43,954 more over 5 years at Synchrony (3.55%) than at the FDIC national average (0.38%), and about $47,514 more than at Chase's 0.01%. But no savings APY in this table is locked: every bank above may change its variable rate at any time (each official page says so). For a truly fixed multi-year return you would need a CD — e.g. Barclays lists a 12-month Online CD at 4.50% APY and a 60-month CD at 4.75% APY (as of 10/07/2026, official compare page) — CDs lock the rate but restrict access and may charge early-withdrawal penalties. Benchmark context: the Federal Reserve raised the federal funds target range to 3.75%–4.00% on September 16, 2026 (federalreserve.gov), so today's top HYSA APYs sit just below the policy-rate range while the average savings account (0.38%, FDIC) sits far below it.
