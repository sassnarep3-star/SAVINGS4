F1 — Capital One product page renders “NaN APY”. The official 360 Performance Savings page displays literally “NaN APY … effective 10/21/2026” (a site data error). The rate used in this study (3.20%) comes from Capital One's official application domain, which states “A rate of 3.20% Annual Percentage Yield (APY) applies to all account balances … effective as of 10/21/2026.” Action for owner: re-check both pages before opening.
capitalone.com product page · apply.capitalone.com
F2 — Citi Accelerate Savings APY could not be verified (left blank). Citi's official savings page renders a template placeholder — “Earn :BLUE_AccSavings_Rate% Annual Percentage Yield” — and its footnote says the APY is accurate as of 10/13/2026 “in the location entered”, with the rate depending on Rate Region, Relationship Tier and promotion, and the account available only in select markets. No APY number could be confirmed from an official Citi page without entering a location, so none is published.
citi.com/banking/savings-account · citi.com/banking/compare-bank-accounts
F3 — Bank of America Advantage Savings APY could not be verified (left blank). BofA's official product page masks its values (it literally renders “$XXXX” for the fee/balance figures and gives no APY in the fetched page), and BofA rates are state-specific. Only the $100 minimum opening deposit and $8 monthly maintenance fee were confirmed, from BofA's official Personal Schedule of Fees (effective August 7, 2026). No APY is published for BofA.
bankofamerica.com — Advantage Savings · Personal Schedule of Fees
F4 — Chase APY is ZIP/market-specific. Chase's official savings page refuses to show a rate table without a 5-digit ZIP (“In order to see rates table, please provide valid 5-digit zip code”). The 0.01% APY used here is from Chase's official Consumer Deposit Rates sheet in effect Friday, October 16, 2026 for the market in that file (rdfl1). Your market may differ — enter your ZIP on chase.com to confirm.
chase.com interest-rates page · rate sheet PDF
F5 — Discover Bank no longer appears to be a separate $250k limit / separate offer. Discover's online-savings URL now serves Capital One's 360 Performance Savings page, and Capital One states that Capital One or Discover Bank deposit accounts opened on or after May 18, 2025 count together for FDIC coverage. Discover is therefore excluded as a separate row to avoid double-counting insurance. Action for owner: if you hold pre-May-2025 Discover deposits, check your exact aggregation with Capital One/FDIC EDIE.
capitalone.com (Discover note in FAQ)
F6 — Wells Fargo 3.25% is conditional and can be withdrawn. It is for new savings customers only, needs ≥$25,000 within 30 days and maintained daily, and Wells states the variable rate “may change at any time”, including to the standard rate or 0%. The standard Platinum/Way2Save rate is 0.01%. Do not plan a multi-year return around 3.25% without re-checking.
wellsfargo.com/savings-cds/rates
F7 — Barclays monthly fee & some funding rules not stated on the official compare page (left blank). Barclays' compare page gives APYs, minimums, maximums and account-count limits but the fetched page did not state a monthly fee for Tiered/Online Savings, so the fee column is blank rather than assumed $0. Also note the same page lists APYs “accurate as of 10/07/2026” — the oldest as-of date in this study.
Barclaysus.com/compare-our-products
F8 — SoFi Plus subscription fee amount not verified. SoFi's official Rate Sheet (Oct 20, 2026) confirms SoFi Plus members earn 4.70% on the first $20,000 / 3.30% above, and refers to a “SoFi Plus Subscription Fee” in separate terms, but the rate sheet itself does not state the amount. The Returns table therefore shows SoFi Plus interest before subscription cost, and the fee amount is left blank.
sofi.com/legal/banking-rate-sheet
F9 — No comparable “security level” score is published. All included banks state FDIC insurance and app/online access on official pages, but there is no official, like-for-like security certification published for every bank, so this study does not rank banks by “security level”. Owners who need that should check each bank's official security centre and confirm FDIC status in BankFind.
FDIC BankFind · Example: Capital One security page
F10 — Excluded: AARP/Barclays Select Savings bonus. A separate Barclays AARP-member page advertises 4.25% APY and a $400 bonus after depositing $40,000 (offer expires 10/31/2026). It is membership-gated, so it is excluded from the main big-bank comparison and flagged here for completeness.
banking.Barclaysus.com/select-home/savings
F11 — Synchrony APY date precision. Synchrony's official page title is “High Yield Savings Accounts – October 2026 Rates” and the page shows 3.55% APY with no minimums/fees; it does not print a day-level “as of” date in the content fetched on 2026-10-22. The APY is treated as current as of the check date (2026-10-22).
synchrony.com/banking/products/high-yield-savings
F12 — All projections assume constant variable APYs. 3- and 5-year interest figures will differ — possibly materially — as banks change rates. The Federal Reserve's next decisions and each bank's rate page must be re-checked periodically. This is a limitation of any HYSA projection, stated here so the tables are not misread as guarantees.
